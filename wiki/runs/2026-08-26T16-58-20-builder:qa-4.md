### Bugs found

1. **Duplicate solve written to history/records — reproduced cleanly, high confidence.** Finish a solve (reach `finished`), then click "Next scramble": the just-finished solve gets written into history/records a **second time** as a new row (new `id`/`createdAt`, identical scramble/solution/moveCount/rawMs). Verified with a controlled before/after count: `solves().length` was 5, then went to 6 after clicking Next Scramble with **no new solve completed in between** — and entry #5 and #6 are field-for-field identical except id/timestamp. This double-occupies rank slots on both boards (explains why Best Single/Fewest Moves briefly showed three identical `0.20 / 21 moves` rows from what were actually two real solves). This reproduction happened in a window with no concurrent HMR edit overlapping it, so I'm confident it's a real defect, not an environment artifact. File: `dist/web/App.tsx` (the `startFresh` / "again" button handler around line 258 is where I'd start looking — it likely re-persists the current finished session on top of whatever already recorded it on reaching solved).

2. **Install banner overlaps and blocks the "Next scramble" button.** `.install-note` and `.again` (Next Scramble) are both `position: absolute`, bottom-anchored with nearly identical offsets (`dist/web/styles.css` lines 459-478 and 515-530), and the note (later in DOM, no z-index) paints on top. `elementFromPoint` on the button's own center returns the banner, not the button — confirmed the button is only clickable via a ~13px unobstructed sliver at its very top edge, not its visual center where a real tap would land. This is exactly the audience the banner targets (plain-Safari-tab, not-installed users), so it's a real risk to the core "finish a solve → start the next one" loop. Note: partway through my session a dismiss "×" appeared on the banner (apparently from a concurrent edit to this same file — see caveat below), and dismissing it does reveal the button; but even in that version the banner still fully covers the button by default until the user discovers and taps the dismiss control, which nothing prompts them to do.

3. **Environmental confound, not an app defect per se:** `dist/web/App.tsx`, `dist/web/components/MenuSheet.tsx`, and this project's `CLAUDE.md` were being actively edited by what looks like another live session throughout my entire QA run (Vite HMR logs show edits every 1-5 minutes from before I started to after I finished). This caused one observed crash ("Rendered more hooks than during the previous render" — React error, blank screen) that cleared cleanly on a manual reload. Given the churn, I'd treat the crash as an HMR artifact rather than a shippable bug, but flag it so nobody is surprised, and recommend a fresh, isolated QA pass once that other work has landed and settled — a couple of my findings (the mid-session appearance of the banner's dismiss button, and one competition-mode DNF solve that landed in history mislabeled as casual/no-penalty) are most simply explained by code changing under me mid-test rather than app logic, and are worth a clean re-check rather than fully trusting.

4. **Minor, not a failure:** the HoldZone two-finger-hold component (`dist/web/components/HoldZone.tsx`) tracks pad state via `useState` + a closure-captured `down` set rather than a functional updater. When I fired two `pointerdown` events in the same synchronous tick (simulating truly simultaneous multi-touch), the second event's stale closure overwrote the first's result and "both down" was never reached; staggering them by even one tick worked correctly and reached `holding`/`solving` as expected. Real touch hardware may or may not deliver two simultaneous contacts within one JS tick, so I can't say this reproduces on a real iPhone, but the pattern is a genuine landmine worth a quick look given this is the one moment the spec cares most about ("the clock starts the moment you lift them, in the same instant").

**Recommendation:** fix #1 (duplicate history write) before shipping — it directly undermines the "honest record of every solve" claim the app is built around. Fix #2 (banner/button overlap) since it blocks the primary loop for exactly the users it's meant to help. Re-run a quick QA pass on Competition-mode penalty display and the install banner once the concurrent edits I observed have settled, since both were in flux during this session.
